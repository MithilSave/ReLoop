| PWM (Amendment) Rules, 2022, incl. **Guidelines on EPR for Plastic Packaging** (notified Feb 2022, Schedule II) | Mandatory EPR targets for plastic packaging, category-wise; centralised online EPR portal run by CPCB; EPR certificates generated by registered Plastic Waste Processors | [REG-V] |

## 2. Obligated entities [REG-V]

The EPR guidelines place obligations on **PIBOs**:
- **Producers**: make plastic packaging, or use it for products they then sell.
- **Importers**: import plastic packaging or packaged products.
- **Brand Owners**: sell goods under a registered brand that use plastic packaging.

Each must register on the CPCB portal (or with the SPCB, depending on its scale and type: [REG-U]) and meet category-wise targets for recycling, reuse and end-of-life disposal.

**[DESIGN]** ReLoop stores `epr_registration_no` and the client's **self-declared** targets. ReLoop does not compute statutory targets.

## 3. Plastic Waste Processors (PWPs) [REG-V]

PWPs, including recyclers, co-processors (e.g. cement kilns), and waste-to-energy and waste-to-oil operators, register on the CPCB portal. **Registered PWPs generate EPR certificates** on the portal for quantities they process, by category and by processing type. PIBOs then obtain these certificates to meet their obligations.

**[DESIGN]** In ReLoop, the recycler is the only party that records processing and certificate references. The `recyclers.processing_type` field distinguishes recycling from end-of-life options, because the guidelines treat them differently against targets.

## 4. Plastic packaging categories [REG-V]

| Category | Description (summary) |
|---|---|
| I | Rigid plastic packaging |
| II | Flexible packaging of single- or multi-layer plastics of different types (e.g. sheets, covers, carry bags, pouches) |
| III | Multi-layered plastic packaging with at least one layer of plastic and at least one layer of non-plastic material |
| IV | Plastic sheets or similar used for packaging, and carry bags made of compostable plastics |

**[DESIGN]** `material_categories.default_packaging_category` (e.g. PET bottles → CAT_I) is only a UI hint. The recycler sets the category on each processing record and certificate reference, because a polymer code does not determine the packaging category.

## 5. Processing evidence

**[REG-U]** The guidelines require PWPs and PIBOs to keep records and file returns on the portal. The exact documents a PWP must keep for each certificate need confirming (Q-03).

**[DESIGN]** ReLoop's evidence package per processing record contains:
- material passport extracts for every input batch (§ see 05 §4.10);
- receipts with weighbridge slips;
- processing input and output and residue;
- measured yield;
- certificate references;
- the NGO decision;
- file hashes for every document.

## 6. Certificate references [DESIGN]

ReLoop stores `certificate_ref`, packaging category, kg, issue date and a copy of the document **as entered by the recycler**. ReLoop does not validate them against the portal, because no public integration is assumed ([REG-U], Q-04). The UI labels them "recycler-declared certificate reference".

## 7. EPR attribution and double-counting safeguards

There are five distinct processes, and ReLoop handles only the first, second and fourth:

| # | Process | Owner | ReLoop role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Physical waste traceability | Operational participants | **Records** (material passport) |
| 2 | Environmental impact verification | NGO | **Hosts** the independent review |
| 3 | Statutory EPR eligibility | Law; PWP and PIBO on the CPCB portal | Stores the recycler's declaration (`eligible_kg`, `eligibility_basis`) only |
| 4 | EPR quantity allocation | Recycler → EPR client (by contract) | **Ledger** with hard caps (05 §6) |
| 5 | Government portal submission | PWP / PIBO | **None.** Exports supporting reports only |

Safeguards:
1. Σ active allocations ≤ `eligible_kg` ≤ measured `output_kg` (DB constraint and row lock).
2. Each certificate reference is unique per recycler. Allocations linked to a certificate cannot exceed its kg.
3. Reversals are new rows. Nothing is deleted.
4. Dashboard totals separate "allocated & NGO-verified" from "allocated & not verified".
5. Forecast or aggregation-plan quantities can **never** be allocated, because allocation only references `processing_records`.
6. **Limit:** ReLoop cannot stop a recycler from also using the same processing quantity off-platform. This is mitigated by stating it in the client report, by NGO review, and by the recycler's contractual declaration (Q-05).

## 8. Compliance boundaries

1. ReLoop is not a government EPR portal. [DESIGN]
2. ReLoop does not issue statutory EPR certificates. Registered PWPs generate them on the CPCB portal. [REG-V]
3. NGO verification is independent supporting evidence with no statutory authority. [REG-V: the guidelines do not, to our knowledge, give NGOs a certification role; Q-06]
4. EPR companies remain responsible for their own obligations and filings. [REG-V]
5. Collected plastic is not automatically eligible for EPR fulfilment. [REG-V]
6. The same eligible quantity cannot be claimed twice. [REG-V principle; ReLoop enforces it within its own ledger]
7. Predicted or aggregated quantities are planning data only and carry no EPR value. [DESIGN]

## 9. EPR company workflow

See 03 §4.5. The output is an **EPR supporting evidence report** (PDF/CSV) per reporting period. It lists allocations, certificate references, source batch lineage, yield, NGO decision status, open discrepancies, and a disclaimer stating §8.
